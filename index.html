About WordCounter

Simple, fast, and free word counting tool

Our Purpose

WordCounter is a free online tool designed to help writers, students, professionals, and anyone who needs to count words and characters quickly and accurately. Whether you're working on an essay, article, social media post, or any text-based content, our tool provides instant, real-time counting to help you stay within your limits and meet your goals.

    Get instant word and character counts as you type

  • No Registration Required

  • 100% Free

  • Works Everywhere
